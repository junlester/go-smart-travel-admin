# Free/Low-Cost SMS Providers

## ⚠️ Reality Check

**Truly free SMS providers don't exist** because:
- SMS carriers charge per message
- Infrastructure costs money
- No provider can offer unlimited free SMS

**However**, there are options with:
- ✅ Free trial credits
- ✅ Very low-cost plans ($1-5/month)
- ✅ Limited free tiers

---

## 🆓 Free Trial Options

### 1. **Twilio** - Free Trial ⭐
**Free Credits:** $15.50 (one-time)
**SMS Count:** ~1,550-2,000 SMS messages
**Cost After Trial:** $0.0075-0.01 per SMS

**Details:**
- No credit card required initially
- Free trial credits expire after some time
- Can send to verified numbers only (trial)
- Philippines: ~$0.01 per SMS

**Best for:** Testing, small projects

**Limitations:**
- One-time only
- Limited to verified numbers (trial)
- Need to upgrade for production

---

### 2. **Vonage** - Free Trial
**Free Credits:** Varies (check website)
**SMS Count:** Depends on credits
**Cost After Trial:** $0.0068 per SMS

**Details:**
- Free trial credits available
- No credit card required (sometimes)
- Can test before paying

**Best for:** Testing Vonage features

---

### 3. **D7 Networks** - Trial Credits
**Free Credits:** Trial credits for new users
**Details:** Check their website for current offers

**Best for:** Testing, evaluation

---

## 💰 Very Low-Cost Options ($1-5/month)

### 1. **Telesign** - $1/month
**Cost:** $1/month
**Includes:** Up to 1,000 API calls/month
**SMS Cost:** Additional per SMS

**Details:**
- $1/month base fee
- Limited to 1,000 API calls
- Additional SMS costs
- Good for SMS verification/2FA

**Best for:** Low volume, verification messages

**Limitations:**
- Limited to 1,000 calls/month
- Additional SMS costs
- May not be cost-effective for regular SMS

---

### 2. **Commio** - Free SMS API
**Cost:** Free (check terms)
**Details:** 
- Claims free SMS API
- Check limitations and terms
- May have restrictions

**Best for:** Very low volume, testing

**Limitations:**
- Unknown limitations
- May have restrictions
- Check terms carefully
